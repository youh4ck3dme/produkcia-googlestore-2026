Privacy Policy

Effective from: July 11, 2026 • BizAgent App • GDPR compliant (EU Regulation 2016/679)

1. Data Controller

The data controller for the BizAgent app is BizAgent s.r.o.
Contact: support@bizagent.sk
GDPR: gdpr@bizagent.sk

2. Data We Process

  • Identification data (name, company ID, tax ID, business address)
  • Invoice and client data
  • Expense and income records
  • Authentication data (email, password – stored in hashed form via Supabase Auth)
  • Receipt photos (uploaded to Supabase Storage; text extracted on-device via Google ML Kit OCR)
  • AI prompts and BizBot chat history (stored in Supabase)
  • Technical data (anonymous analytics events via Firebase Analytics)

3. Purpose of Processing

  • Providing app services (invoicing, expense tracking)
  • AI assistance (data analysis, response generation)
  • Subscription and in-app purchase management
  • Communication with users
  • Security and service improvement
  • Legal compliance

4. Legal Basis

  • User consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)
  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Legitimate interest of the controller (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for the duration of app use. Accounting documents per legal retention periods (up to 10 years). After account deletion, all data is permanently removed.

6. Sharing with Third Parties

  • Supabase Inc. (EU, eu-central-1) – authentication, database, file storage, Edge Functions
  • Mistral AI and Google (Gemini) – AI prompt processing (only when you actively use AI features)
  • Google ML Kit – on-device text recognition from receipt photos
  • Google Firebase Analytics – anonymous usage statistics
  • Apple App Store / Google Play – payment processing for subscriptions and in-app purchases
  • Google Firebase / Firestore (limited legacy) – IČO lookup cache, expense categorization
  • icoatlas.sk (optional) – company registry lookups
  • Slovak authorities – only when legally required

We do not sell your data to third parties for marketing purposes.

7. AI Features – Special Notice

AI features (BizBot, tax assistant, email generator, analytics) process your text queries and relevant business context via a Supabase Edge Function, which forwards prompts to Mistral AI (primary) or Google Gemini (fallback). BizBot chat history is stored in Supabase until account deletion. AI responses are informational only and do not replace professional advice.

8. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

The app offers optional paid plans (sub_pro_monthly, sub_pro_year, sub_business_monthly, one_time_starter) processed through Apple App Store or Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled entirely by the platform store; BizAgent does not store payment card data.

9. Your Rights and Account Deletion

  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the Slovak Data Protection Authority

You can delete your account:

  • In-app: Settings → "Delete account and all data" (immediate)
  • By email: support@bizagent.sk
  • Web: https://bizagent.sk/delete-account.html

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL), stored on Supabase servers in the EU (eu-central-1), protected by authentication and Row Level Security (RLS). Edge Functions require a valid authenticated session.

11. Contact

Email: gdpr@bizagent.sk
General contact: support@bizagent.sk

We respond to requests within 30 days.
